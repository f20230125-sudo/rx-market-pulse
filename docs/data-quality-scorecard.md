## Notes

- **C01**: 36,110,354 rows across 2019-2025.
- **C03**: Placeholder rows ('** FROM CL') and codes that lost leading zeros. They never reach the facts; the dollars on them are blank (suppressed), so the market total is unaffected.
- **C04**: Reversals and adjustments are netted by the source before publication.
- **C05**: Source rounding. Total amount is used throughout; the Medicaid/non-Medicaid split is only shown separately.
- **C07**: A duplicate would double-count spend.
- **C08**: The product list is a quarterly snapshot; the latest snapshot per NDC is kept.
- **C09**: Cells with fewer than 11 prescriptions are blanked in the state rows but counted in the national row. National totals therefore come from XX; state shares are shares of reported (unsuppressed) spend.
- **C10**: Suppressed rows carry no units or dollars. They are kept (not dropped) so row counts reconcile.
- **C11**: Dollars on NDCs the list does not know are reported as 'unmatched', never guessed.
- **C12**: The directory lists current products, so discontinued NDCs seen in older Medicaid years are missing. Matching on the nine-digit labeler+product code recovers packs the directory does not list.
- **C13**: Salts and hydrates are stripped and combinations sorted so one molecule has one key.
- **C14**: Area comes from the FDA pharmacologic class, then the same ingredient, then an editable name list (data/ref/). Unclassified spend is shown as its own bar in every chart, never spread across other areas.
- **C15**: Case and punctuation variants merge by rule; completed acquisitions are mapped by an explicit, tested list.
- **C16**: Names such as 'LEVOTHYROX' are not used. Product identity comes from the NDC, joined to the product list.
- **C17**: These rows stay in the facts with an empty product; charts group them as 'Unmatched'.
- **C18**: 2026 is a partial year in the source and is left out of annual comparisons on purpose.
